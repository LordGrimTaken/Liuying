01Features

Everything a media center should be, shaped around the things you already watch.

Bring your own sources

Liuying is a blank scroll. Add the sources you choose and your library gathers in one place, with nothing preloaded.

Artwork and ratings

Posters, backdrops, summaries and ratings are filled in automatically, so browsing feels like a media center.

Subtitles done right

Pick your language, adjust size, color and timing, and let the player remember your preference.

Your place saved everywhere

Stop mid-scene and resume from the exact second. Your watch position is always kept.

Fast native player

A native Android player with hardware decoding keeps playback smooth and start times short.

Free and open source

No ads, no accounts required to try it. Built in the open under GPL-3.0.

02Install on Android

Five quick steps. No store needed.

  1. Download the APK

    Tap the download button on this page to get the latest Liuying APK.

  2. Allow install from this source

    If Android asks, open Settings and allow installs from your browser or file manager.

  3. Open the file

    Open the downloaded file from your notifications or Downloads folder.

  4. Tap Install

    Confirm the prompt and wait a moment while Liuying installs.

  5. Open Liuying

    Launch the app, add your sources, and start watching.

04Questions

The short answers.

Does Liuying host any content?

No. Liuying does not host or provide any content. Sources are added by you or come from third parties, and you are responsible for what you add.

Is it free?

Yes. Liuying is free and licensed under GPL-3.0, so you can read, change and share the source code.
